How to prepare

One pot of boiling water

  1. Boil waterBring 3500–4000 cc of water to a rolling boil.
  2. Add from frozenNo need to fully thaw. Cut the bag and pour the block in.
  3. StirKeep the heat high and stir to dissolve.
  4. Lower the heatWhen it starts to foam, reduce slightly to keep boiling without overflowing. Add sugar now if desired.
  5. Boil 5–10 minutesKeep at a full boil to bring out the aroma. Ready to serve.
