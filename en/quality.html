Full commitment to food safety・Quality first・Customer satisfaction

Our food safety policy

ISO 22000HACCPNON-GMONo PreservativesMade in Taiwan

From bean selection to delivery: non-GMO designated soybean varieties from Canada and the US, incoming inspection, automated grinding, high-temperature sterilization and flash freezing. Every pack carries its expiry date on the seal.

Our pancakes and pies use SQF-certified flour, are hand-rolled and contain no artificial colors. No preservatives in any product, with regular third-party testing.

  • ISO 22000 Food Safety Management System
  • HACCP certified production
  • SGS non-GMO soybean testing (Pure Happiness series)
  • Intertek tests: aflatoxin & pesticide residue
  • Intertek tests: preservatives & microbiology
  • Product liability insurance NT$20 million (Fubon)
