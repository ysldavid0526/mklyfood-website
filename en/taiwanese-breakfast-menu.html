How to Add a Taiwanese Breakfast Menu to Your Café or Tea Shop

Mikely foods, Taiwanese soy milk manufacturer since 2002・Updated 2026-10-23

Quick answer
Start with three items that need only a pot and a pan: hot or iced soy milk, scallion pancakes with egg, and douhua. Frozen concentrates and frozen pancakes keep prep simple and consistent.

The classic Taiwanese breakfast

  • Soy milk (dou jiang): sweet, unsweetened, or savory with vinegar and soy sauce.
  • Rice & peanut milk (mi jiang): nutty and comforting.
  • Scallion pancake (zhua bing): flaky layers, often with egg and cheese.
  • Douhua: silky tofu pudding with syrup and toppings.

FAQ

What do Taiwanese breakfast shops serve?
Typical items are soy milk (sweet or savory), rice and peanut milk, scallion pancakes (zhua bing), egg crepes (dan bing) and douhua.
Do I need a soy milk machine?
No. With frozen soy milk concentrate you only need a large pot: boil water, add the frozen block and boil 5–10 minutes.
Can I make douhua with the same soy milk?
Yes. Use a little less water to make thicker soy milk, then set it with gypsum or GDL.
