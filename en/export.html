Export FAQ

Do you export?
We welcome inquiries from importers and distributors worldwide. Contact us with your country and requirements and we will confirm export terms.
What is the minimum order quantity?
MOQ depends on the product mix and shipping method. Tell us your estimated volume and we will advise.
How are products shipped?
All products are frozen and must be shipped and stored at -18°C, typically by reefer container.
Can I get samples?
Yes, sample arrangements can be discussed after your inquiry.

We currently hold ISO 22000 and HACCP. Please ask about any other certification your market requires.
